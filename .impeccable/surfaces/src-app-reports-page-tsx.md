# Reports workspace

## Scope and visitor mode
Operate. `/reports` is an evidence workstation for GMs, QMs, and engineers under audit pressure. Not a marketing surface and not a new visual world — inherit Geist, CSS variables, burnt-orange accent for status/print only.

## Audience, job, proof
Plant quality staff must generate a Financial Year (1 Apr–31 Mar) or custom Date of Entry extract from the append-only ledger. Proof is deterministic analytics over `recordedAt`, identical preview and print models, and explicit unavailable metrics.

## Chosen direction
Exclusive report types (FY Audit Pack, Fundamentals, Stage, Defect, Size). Local report scope that does not mutate Dashboard filters. Date basis is Date of Entry only. Forensic book quarantined.

## Memorable moment
Exact inclusive dates under the FY selector, the words “Date of Entry,” and a coverage strip that can refuse export when there are no qualifying records.

## Unresolved
Second custom-range date basis (company confirmation). Plant-wide accepted quantity (company confirmation). Checked-participation % across sequential gates (not invented).
